How it works

Three beats, like a waltz

  1. The music plays

    Your speaker, a friend’s turntable, the kitchen radio, the sound system at the bar. If the room can hear it, so can Kairosong: the app knows millions of songs.

  2. Kairosong listens in

    A few seconds through one phone’s mic is all it takes to know the song.

  3. Everyone guesses

    Memory, ear or a wild hunch: each player sets a year on their own phone. Before every reveal, the app drops a curious or defining fact from that year without naming it: “The song came out the year when…”, then comes the answer: the year, the title, the artist, the cover…

Explore

After the year, the story.

The answer lands and curiosity kicks in: tap Explore, and the app tells you about the song, the album, the artist. Enough to get the table talking before the next song.

One more tap finds the song on your music service. And in your notebook, every song keeps its story: swipe from one to the next.

Sonar · Solo

On your own, and a song catches your ear? Open the Sonar.

At the café, at a friend’s, by the radio: one tap, and the Sonar listens to whatever’s playing. No game to set up, no QR, nobody to wait for. You set your year - or not - the answer lands, and Explore tells you about the song, the album, the artist.

The Sonar is Kairosong played solo. Shazam gives you the title; the Sonar makes you guess the year first, then keeps every song in your notebook: food for a music lover’s curiosity, and a record of your memories in sound.

What stays

The morning after, the playlist’s still there

Star a song during the game and it’ll be waiting in your notebook. Every night is kept there, song by song, along with what the Sonar found: on your phone and nowhere else. New phone? Export your notebook and it comes with you.

FAQ

  • What if it’s a live, a remix or a cover?

    A live recording, a remaster, a reissue won’t make the song any younger: Billie Jean live in 1988 is still 1982. A cover by someone else is another story, with its own year. So is a remix, unless the table switches to source mode: then you guess the year of the original song. If it wasn’t the original version playing, the reveal says so, and names the one you heard.

  • Is it like Hitster?

    Same family: you’re working out the year of a song. But there are no cards and no set playlist here. Kairosong listens to your own music and knows millions of songs.

  • Song not recognized?

    The MC (whoever runs the game) listens again or moves on to the next one. Nobody loses points. Kairosong struggles with classical music, obscure remixes, and anything that was never officially released.

  • What if I know nothing about music?

    It’s less about knowing dates than remembering. The best players aren’t always the music buffs. And you pick up the rules as you play: the app slips them in one at a time while you wait.

  • What do I need?

    A phone for the MC, one per player, and any music source: a speaker, a turntable, the radio. It runs in the browser, on iPhone and Android. No Spotify, no app to install, no account.

  • How many players?

    On your own, fire up the Sonar: the app listens, you guess. Two makes a duel. Ten makes a party. One phone each, as many as you like.

  • What about my data?

    No account, no email: your name is temporary and goes away with the game. Everything else is on the privacy page.

The name

Kairos. The Greeks had two words for time. Chronos, the kind that ticks by. Kairos, the kind that counts: the right moment, the one you know when it comes.

A song often holds one. Kairosong goes looking for it.
